Data sources

Draft (chat) — from docs/data-sources.md. The author’s edit is in Week 20.

  • Tiger population. National Tiger Conservation Authority and Wildlife Institute of India, All India Tiger Estimation reports, 2006–2022 (https://ntca.gov.in). Government of India. The census series of this project is the within-reserve estimate of 2014, 2018 and 2022.
  • Reserve and corridor outlines. National Tiger Conservation Authority (2022), PA_TR_Corridor_Final (KML), Decision Support System (https://ntca.gov.in/dss/). Government of India; attribution required. The outlines are the core protected areas, not the notified area of the reserve.
  • Reserve area. The NTCA-notified core and buffer total of each reserve.
  • Tiger records. GBIF.org (2026-09-07), GBIF Occurrence Download, https://doi.org/10.15468/dl.npxmxx. CC BY or CC0 for each record.
  • Mammal records (the background). GBIF.org (2026-09-07), GBIF Occurrence Download, https://doi.org/10.15468/dl.2d523e. CC BY or CC0 for each record.
  • Land cover. ESA WorldCover 2021 v200, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7254221. CC BY 4.0.
  • Human pressure. Global Human Modification 2022 (Theobald et al., v3), https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14502573. CC BY 4.0.
  • Roads, settlements and zoos. OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), from the Geofabrik India extract (https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/india.html).
  • Terrain. AWS Terrain Tiles (https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/).
  • States. Natural Earth admin-1 boundaries (public domain).
  • Districts. DataMeet Census-2011 districts (CC BY 4.0).
  • Forest (a cross-check of the reserve area only). Forest Survey of India, India State of Forest Report 2021 (https://fsi.nic.in).
